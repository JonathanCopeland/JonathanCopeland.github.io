# OPES Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 29, 2025

## Overview
OPES ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles information.

## Information We Collect
### Information You Provide:
- Investment transaction data (stock symbols, quantities, purchase prices, dates)
- Account names and descriptions you create
- App preferences and settings

### Information We Don't Collect:
- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- Banking or brokerage account credentials
- Location data
- Device contacts, photos, or other personal files
- Advertising identifiers

## How We Use Information
The information you enter is used solely to:
- Display your investment portfolio
- Calculate gains and losses
- Provide real-time stock price updates
- Save your app preferences

## Data Storage and Security
- **Local Storage:** All your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's secure SwiftData framework
- **No Cloud Storage:** We do not store your data on our servers
- **No Data Transmission:** Your personal investment data is never transmitted to us or third parties

## Third-Party Services
OPES uses the following third-party services:
- **Yahoo Finance API:** To retrieve real-time stock prices and market data (public information only)
- **Currency Exchange APIs:** To provide currency conversion rates (no personal data shared)

## Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Your investment data remains private and is only accessible to you on your device.

## Your Rights
You have complete control over your data:
- All data can be deleted by removing the app
- Individual transactions and accounts can be deleted within the app
- No account registration is required

## Children's Privacy
OPES is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
